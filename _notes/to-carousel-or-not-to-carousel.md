title: To carousel or not to carousel
---
That is the question. 

I posted a handful of photos on [[putters-new-garage]], and have plenty more I'd like to post. But, it's cluttering the reading space.

I'd love to have a better way to display multiple images. I attempted [Jekyll's Slider/Carousel component](https://jekyllcodex.org/without-plugin/slider/) but had little luck. It wants me to utilize the carousel component within the `_layouts/page.html` file which doesn't quite work because each post and page will want carousels in different locations.

I want to have granular control over where the images are within my post.

I tried to use Claude 3.5 Sonnet to help me create Ruby generators as well as custom build logic but had little success.

I think for now, I'll place this on the back burner and focus on other areas.
